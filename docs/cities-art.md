# مدن اليمن | Art inventory

Taiz, Ibb, Old Sana’a, Shibam and the coast are playable interpretations on the game's local road graph. The route length and building elevations are game coordinates.

The four quarter_*.jpg elevation maps are original AI-generated diffuse art composed for this game, cropped and assembled into floors offline. Taiz and Ibb share the basalt material; Ibb has its own scenery and weather. No third-party photograph was embedded. Close-range architectural trim, trees, street signs and the Sabir road mesh are drawn by project code. See ASSETS.md for inherited assets and their licenses.
