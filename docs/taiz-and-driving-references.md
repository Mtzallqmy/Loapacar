# Taiz scene and driving references

This update uses original in-game models and a fictionalized street layout. Photographs served as visual reference.

- [Old City of Taiz photos](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Old_City_of_Taiz) informed the compact frontage, varied roof edges, and differences in height across adjoining buildings.
- [Taiz and Al-Qahira Castle photographs](https://thevelvetrocket.com/2015/01/04/visiting-taiz-yemen/) informed the fort silhouette and the siting of the landmark above a slope. The model is inspired by the reference and has fictional dimensions.
- [Old City of Sana'a, UNESCO photographic gallery](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/385/gallery/) gives a reference for colored openings above recessed openings, dense building masses, and white gypsum against warm masonry.
- [Yemeni agricultural terraces, including Ibb](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Agricultural_terraces_in_Yemen) provide regional art references for how slopes are broken into discrete steps.
- [NASA photograph of Aden's bay and Crater district](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Port_of_Aden,_Yemen_from_ISS.jpg) provides a reference for the relationship between the rocky district, sheltered bay, and adjoining waterfront.
- [CARLA Agents](https://carla.readthedocs.io/en/latest/adv_agents/) informed the separation of steering and speed decisions, and the priority given to traffic control and obstacles.
- [SUMO car-following](https://sumo.dlr.de/docs/Car-Following-Models.html) informed the use of headway and braking assumptions to constrain speed. The tuning applies only to the game's physics, road geometry and traffic.
