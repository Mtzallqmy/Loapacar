# Open World visual inventory

The four `quarter_mahwit.jpg`, `quarter_marib.jpg`, `quarter_tihama.jpg`, and `quarter_ibb.jpg` textures are original synthetic diffuse facade artwork generated for this project and cropped to individual 640-pixel offline game textures. They represent regional artistic inspiration and do not reproduce measured buildings. No outside photographs, logos, or third-party models were embedded by this asset update. Inherited assets and source references are recorded in [cities-art.md](cities-art.md) and [ASSETS.md](ASSETS.md).

The route is a fictional connected 22 km model; named districts, landmarks, distances, and buildings are not surveyed maps. Mahwit and Marib add authored junction positions to the same collision, rendering, and navigation grid. Hodeidah adds a playable passenger itinerary, while Ibb now has a distinct facade texture.
