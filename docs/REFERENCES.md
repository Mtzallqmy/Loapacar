# Architectural and vehicle reference notes

These references informed procedural art direction. They do not turn the fictional street layout into a geographic reconstruction, establish model licensing or validate the physics.

- UNESCO, Old City of Sana'a: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/385 . Tower houses, stone ground floors, brick and white gypsum details. Used to separate Sana'a facade treatment from generic glass buildings.
- UNESCO, Old Walled City of Shibam: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/192/ . Dense, tall mud-brick houses and grid streets. Used for relative tower heights and earth-toned treatment, not a traced city plan.
- UNESCO, Historic Town of Zabid: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/611/ . Brick courtyard houses and narrow alleys, often lower than the tower cities. Used for the lower-rise Tihama profile and courtyard walls.
- Al Ain, 30 September 2024: https://al-ain.com/article/homes-aden-architectural-paintings . Reporting on Aden's wooden window screens and mixed architectural traditions. Used for coastal facade shutters/screens, not all of Aden's varied architecture.
- BBC Travel: https://www.bbc.com/travel/article/20211004-yemens-ancient-soaring-skyscraper-cities . Architectural reporting and photographs covering tower houses, white lime decoration and traditional construction. Supporting context, not measurements for the game.
- Best Selling Cars Blog, 1 August 2025: https://bestsellingcarsblog.com/2025/08/yemen-h1-2025/ . Reports Hilux and Land Cruiser/pickup strength in Yemen's 2025 new-car sales. This is not the entire on-road fleet or a basis for claiming any exact market-share mix in the game. The game uses unbranded utility/pickup/minibus archetypes, not replicas or licensed models.

Reference pages were read during the November 2026 development pass. Geometry, garment shapes, engine harmonics, road grids and driving parameters are authored approximations. No real engine recording or surveyed street dataset is included in this pass.
